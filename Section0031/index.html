For the Pali Canon, I have used the Thai edition published in Bangkok by Mahāmakut Rājavidyālaya Press and the BUDSIR CD-ROM version prepared by Mahidol University; the European edition edited by Hermann Oldenberg and published in England by the Pali Text Society; and the version of the Sri Lankan edition made available online by the Journal of Buddhist Ethics. For readings from the Sixth Council Burmese edition, I have relied on the help of Thomas Patton.
關於巴利《聖典》，我使用了在曼谷的 Mahāmakut Rājavidyālaya Press 出版的泰文版和瑪希隆大學（Mahidol University）製作的 BUDSIR 光碟版；赫爾曼·奧爾登堡（Hermann Oldenberg）編輯、巴利文獻協會（Pali Text Society）在英格蘭出版的歐洲版；以及《佛教倫理學雜誌》（Journal of Buddhist Ethics）提供的斯里蘭卡版在線版本。至於第六次結集緬甸文版的拼讀，我依靠了托馬斯·巴頓（Thomas Patton）的幫助。
For the Pali commentaries, I have used the Thai editions of the Samantapāsādikā, the Sāratthadīpanī, and the Atthayojanā published in Bangkok by Mahāmakut Rājavidyālaya Press; the Thai edition of the Vimati-vinodanī published in Bangkok by the Bhūmibalo Bhikkhu Foundation Press; the PTS edition of the Samantapāsādikā, edited by J. Takakusu, Makoto Nagai, and Kogen Mizuno; the PTS edition of the Kaṅkhā-vitaraṇī, edited by Dorothy Maskell; the Harvard Oriental Series’ edition of the Visuddhimagga, edited by Henry Clarke Warren and Dharmananda Kosambi; and the Thai edition of the Kaṅkhā-vitaraṇī-purāṇa-ṭīkā and the Kaṅkhā-vitaraṇ-abhinava-ṭīkā published in Bangkok by Mahāchulālongkorn Rājavidyālaya. 至於巴利註釋書，我使用了由 Mahāmakut Rājavidyālaya Press 在曼谷出版的泰文版 Samantapāsādikā 、 Sāratthadīpanī 和 Atthayojanā ；Bhūmibalo Bhikkhu Foundation Press 在曼谷出版的 Vimati-vinodanī ； PTS 版的 Samantapāsādikā ，由 J. Takakusu、Makoto Nagai 和 Kogen Mizuno 編輯； PTS 版的 Kaṅkhā-vitaraṇī ，由 Dorothy Maskell 編輯；哈佛東方叢書（Harvard Oriental Series）版的《清淨道論》（ Visuddhimagga ），由亨利·克拉克·沃倫（Henry Clarke Warren）和達摩南達·科桑比（Dharmananda Kosambi）編輯；由 Mahāchulālongkorn Rājavidyālaya 在曼谷出版的泰文版 Kaṅkhā-vitaraṇi-purāṇa-ṭīkā 和 Kaṅkhā-vitaraṇ-abhinava-ṭīkā 。
