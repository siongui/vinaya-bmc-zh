          Cloth Requisites

     - .. container:: mx-2 is-size-3 is-italic has-text-weight-bold

          衣資具

   * - A bhikkhu has four primary requisites—robe-cloth, food, lodgings, and medicine—and a variety of secondary ones. This and the following five chapters discuss requisites that are allowable and not, along with the proper use of allowable requisites. The suttas provide a background for these discussions by highlighting the proper attitudes that a bhikkhu should develop toward his requisites: He should reflect on their role, not as ends in themselves, but as mere tools toward the training of the mind; and he should develop an attitude of contentment with whatever requisites he receives.

     - 比丘有四種主要資具——衣、食、住處、藥——以及多種次要資具。本章及隨後的五章將探討哪些資具是允許使用的，哪些是不允許的，並如何適當地使用那些允許的資具。經文為這些討論提供了背景，強調了比丘在對待資具時應培養的正確態度：他應當省察資具的作用，視其為心修行的工具，而非目的本身；同時，無論獲得何種資具，他都應培養知足的心態。

   * - .. container:: mx-2

          “And what are the effluents to be abandoned by using? There is the case where a bhikkhu, reflecting appropriately, uses robe-cloth simply to counteract cold, to counteract heat, to counteract the touch of flies, mosquitoes, wind, sun, and reptiles; simply for the purpose of covering the parts of the body that cause shame.

     - .. container:: mx-2

          「那麼，透過使用而應棄除的煩惱\ :small:`[漏]`\ 有哪些呢？即：比丘如理省察，使用衣物僅僅是為了抵禦寒冷、炎熱，抵禦蚊蠅、風、陽光及爬蟲的侵擾；僅僅是為了遮蔽身體羞恥部位。

   * - .. container:: mx-2

          “Reflecting appropriately, he uses almsfood, not playfully, nor for intoxication, nor for putting on bulk, nor for beautification; but simply for the survival and continuance of this body, for ending its afflictions, for the support of the holy life, thinking, ‘Thus will I destroy old feelings (of hunger) and not create new feelings (from overeating). I will maintain myself, be blameless, and live in comfort.’

     - .. container:: mx-2

          「如理省察，他受用施食：既非為了嬉戲，亦非為了沉醉、增肌或美化；而僅是為了維持此色身的存續、消除身體的苦痛，並以此支持梵行生活。思惟：『以此，我將消除舊有的受（飢餓感），而不引發新的受（因過量進食）；我將維持生命，保持無過失，並安適地生活。』

   * - .. container:: mx-2

          “Reflecting appropriately, he uses lodging simply to counteract cold, to counteract heat, to counteract the touch of flies, mosquitoes, wind, sun, and reptiles; simply for protection from the inclemencies of weather and for the enjoyment of seclusion.

     - .. container:: mx-2

          「如理省察，他使用住處僅是為了抵禦寒暑，防避蚊蠅、風吹、日曬及爬蟲的侵擾；僅是為了抵禦惡劣天氣，並享受隱居獨處之樂。
